Common questions

What nisab does this use?

It uses the silver nisab of 52.5 tola, which is the figure many scholars use for cash. Enter today's silver price per tola to get the amount in rupees.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It is a calculation aid. For your own situation, please ask a qualified scholar.
