How to use

  1. Choose a photo from your phone or computer.
  2. Move the quality slider. Lower quality gives a smaller file.
  3. Optionally set a maximum width, for example 1280.
  4. Tap Download to save the compressed photo.

Common questions

How do I make a photo under 100 KB?

Lower the quality to about 40 to 60 and set the maximum width to 800 or 1000. Check the new size shown below the preview.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is processed inside your browser and is never sent to a server.
