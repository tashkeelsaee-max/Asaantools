Common questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the loan, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

Is this the exact bank figure?

It is a close estimate. Banks may add processing fees or use slightly different rounding.
