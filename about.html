About Asaan Tools

Asaan Tools is a small set of free tools for everyday jobs: shrinking a photo for a form, turning pictures into a PDF, counting words, working out a loan instalment, converting marla and kanal, or calculating zakat.

The tools are built to be quick on a phone, to work in Urdu and English, and to keep your files on your own device. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Results are for guidance. For legal, financial or religious decisions, please consult a qualified professional.
