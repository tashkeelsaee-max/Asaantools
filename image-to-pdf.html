    How to use

    1. Choose one or more photos. They are placed in the order you pick them.
    2. Tap Create PDF.
    3. Tap Download PDF to save the file.

    Common questions

    Can I put many photos in one PDF?

    Yes. Select several photos at once. Each photo becomes one page.

    Is it safe for CNIC copies?

    The files are converted inside your browser and are not sent to any server.
